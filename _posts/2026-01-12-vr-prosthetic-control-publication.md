  <p class="lead">
    Dr. Yunmei Liu is the first author of “Virtual Reality as a Platform for Upper-Limb Prosthetic Control Modes Evaluation and Early-Stage Design,” published on January 12, 2026, in <i>IEEE Transactions on Neural Systems and Rehabilitation Engineering</i>.
  </p>

  <h2>Evaluating Prosthetic Interfaces in Virtual Reality</h2>
  <p>
    The collaborative study compared three prosthetic control modes in physical-device and virtual-reality settings, examining task performance, cognitive workload, and usability.
  </p>
  <p>
    The results show that the suitability of VR depends on the control mode and available sensory feedback. Pattern-recognition control produced consistent performance across settings, while other modes showed differences in performance or workload. Usability ratings were broadly consistent. The work supports VR as a useful platform for early design evaluation while identifying opportunities to improve realism, including haptic feedback. It informs Dr. Liu’s continuing research on human-centered prosthetic interfaces and assistive technologies.
  </p>
