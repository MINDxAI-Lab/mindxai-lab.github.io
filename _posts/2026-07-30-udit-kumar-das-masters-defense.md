  <p class="lead">
    The MINDxAI Lab congratulates Udit Kumar Das on successfully defending his master's thesis in Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Louisville.
  </p>

  <h2>A Milestone for the MINDxAI Lab</h2>
  <p>
    Udit joined the lab in Fall 2025 and conducted thesis research on the physiological patterns of novice drivers when they encounter aggressive driving conditions. His work was supported by a University of Louisville Graduate Student Council research grant and contributed to the lab’s broader research on driver safety and human-centered transportation systems.
  </p>
  <p>
    Udit is the first master's student advised by Dr. Yunmei Liu to complete and defend a thesis through the MINDxAI Lab. He will remain with the lab to continue his research as a Ph.D. student.
  </p>
