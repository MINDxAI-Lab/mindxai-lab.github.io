    <strong>Date and Time:</strong> Thursday, August 20, 2026, at 12:30 PM Eastern Time
  </p>

  <h2>About the Talk</h2>
  <p>
    As automated vehicles continue to advance, human-driven and automated vehicles are likely to coexist in mixed traffic for an extended period. Dr. Liu's talk presented three studies examining human responses in this evolving environment:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Situation awareness and automation:</strong> Changes in situation awareness across different levels of automation.</li>
    <li><strong>Surrounding automated-vehicle behavior:</strong> Driver responses to aggressive behaviors of surrounding automated vehicles.</li>
    <li><strong>Human internal states:</strong> The roles of cognitive workload and emotion in human performance.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>
    Together, these studies highlighted the dynamic relationships among automation, surrounding traffic, and human internal states in mixed-traffic systems.
  </p>
