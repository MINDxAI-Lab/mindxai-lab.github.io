Their <em>Fatigue Monitoring</em> project brought physiological sensing and data analysis into a summer research experience for educators.

  <h2>From Sensor Signals to Research Questions</h2>
  <p>
    Working with EmotiBit physiological data, the teachers progressed from visualizing signals to extracting features and comparing them with logged task events. Their research explored skin conductance and heart-rate variability in the context of fatigue monitoring. They prepared a research poster and shared their final presentation slides with Dr. Liu on July 29.
  </p>

  <h2>Connecting Research and Teaching</h2>
  <p>
    The RET Site pairs laboratory research with lesson planning and curriculum development. Mentoring teachers through this process supports connections between university research and classroom learning, giving educators direct experience with the questions, tools, and interpretation involved in engineering research.
  </p>
