    <p class="lead">
      During summer 2024, Dr. Yunmei Liu mentored Emily Montgomery, a University of Louisville undergraduate studying computer science, through the NSF REU Site in Advanced Manufacturing and Supply Chain. The ten-week research experience was part of the program led by Dr. Faisal Aqlan under NSF Award No. 2244119.
    </p>
  </div>

  <h2>From Signal Processing to a Final Report</h2>
  <p>
    Emily worked on Python-based processing and visualization of electromyography (EMG) signals and prepared a research poster. The work connected computing skills with questions about wearable sensing and human performance, giving her practice in organizing analysis code and communicating a research project.
  </p>
  <p>
    On July 31, Emily submitted her final report following Dr. Liu's review and feedback. The summer mentorship supported both technical development and the process of turning research work into a clear written account.
  </p>
  <p>
    Alongside individual projects, the REU program offers workshops, research presentations, and industry visits to connect research with professional practice.
  </p>
  <p>
    Aqlan Lab's public 2024 project description, <em>Fatigue Monitoring in Advanced Manufacturing and Supply Chain Using Wearable Technology</em>, identifies Dr. Liu as the faculty supervisor and outlines research on wearable EMG and forearm muscle fatigue. Its REU program page also lists her as a project mentor.
  </p>
