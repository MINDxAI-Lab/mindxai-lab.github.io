  <p class="lead">
    On June 25, 2026, high school students explored several sides of industrial and systems engineering during <em>Think Like an Engineer: The Ultimate Systems Challenge Camp</em> at the University of Louisville. The one-day summer camp included a morning visit to the MINDxAI Lab.
  </p>
  <h2>A Morning at the Driving Simulator</h2>
  <p>
    Dr. Yunmei Liu and the lab team hosted the Driving Simulator activity, welcoming students into the lab's research environment. Gathered around the simulator, students encountered a hands-on example of how engineering can study the interaction between people and technology. The visit connected a familiar everyday activity&mdash;driving&mdash;with the research tools used to explore human factors in transportation.
  </p>
  <h2>A Full Day of Engineering Exploration</h2>
  <p>
    Beyond MINDxAI's session, the camp's morning program included human&ndash;AI interaction. Afternoon activities turned to logistics and research lab tours. Together, these experiences introduced students to the breadth of ISE, with the driving simulator serving as one stop in a day of practical engineering exploration.
  </p>
