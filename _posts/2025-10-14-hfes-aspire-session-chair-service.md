  <p class="lead">
    On October 14, 2025, Dr. Yunmei Liu served as Session Chair for <strong>A1: Bridging Aging and Technology: HRI and VR Applications</strong> at <a href="https://www.hfes.org/Events/ASPIRE-International-Annual-Meeting/About-the-Meeting/About-the-2025-Meeting-">ASPIRE &mdash; the HFES 69th International Annual Meeting</a> in Chicago, Illinois.
  </p>

  <p class="hfes-service-details">
    <strong>Role:</strong> Session Chair<br>
    <strong>Date and Time:</strong> Tuesday, October 14, 2025, 11:30 AM&ndash;12:30 PM Central Daylight Time (Chicago)<br>
    <strong>Room:</strong> Grand Hall L
  </p>

  <h2>Bridging Aging and Technology</h2>
  <p>
    The session brought together three presentations exploring how human-robot interaction (HRI) and virtual reality (VR) can address the needs of people across age groups. Topics included:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>Interactions with collaborative robots and human colleagues across different age groups.</li>
    <li>Assistive robots that support social engagement for older adults.</li>
    <li>Inclusive virtual reality design and accessibility barriers for older adults.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>
    This professional service contributed to the exchange of research on aging, accessibility, and human-centered technology within the HFES community.
  </p>
