  <p class="lead">
    Dr. Yunmei Liu is a co-author of “Cognitive Workload Assessment in Procedural Tasks,” published online in <i>Human Factors</i> on September 2, 2026. The collaborative paper is first-authored by Niosh Basnet.
  </p>

  <h2>Understanding Workload as Tasks Unfold</h2>
  <p>
    The study examined 30 participants completing cooking tasks under routine conditions and conditions involving disruptions and time pressure. Researchers combined self-reported workload with eye and skin-conductance measures to examine how demands changed during the tasks.
  </p>
  <p>
    Individual disruptions could produce brief increases in workload, while accumulating anomalies could sustain higher demands. The physiological measures differed in their sensitivity, with blink rate providing a useful indicator in this task setting. These findings can inform assistance that responds to both immediate difficulties and the cumulative demands of procedural work.
  </p>
