  <p class="lead">
    Dr. Yunmei Liu co-authored “Effect of interface design on cognitive workload in unmanned aerial vehicle control” with first author Wenjuan Zhang and David B. Kaber. The paper was published online in the <i>International Journal of Human-Computer Studies</i> on May 16, 2024.
  </p>

  <h2>Designing Interfaces with Operator Workload in Mind</h2>
  <p>
    The study evaluates how UAV interfaces developed using the Modified GEDIS-UAV design tool affect cognitive workload. By comparing baseline, enhanced, and degraded interfaces under different vehicle speeds, the research connects design guidance with operator responses under changing task demands.
  </p>
  <p>
    The enhanced interface helped manage workload and moderate the effects of increased task demand. Combining subjective and physiological assessments, the findings support the use of structured interface-design guidance early in UAV system development and point to opportunities for further collaboration on human-centered unmanned systems.
  </p>
