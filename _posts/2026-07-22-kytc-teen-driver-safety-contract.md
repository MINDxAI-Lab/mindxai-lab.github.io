  <p>
    The MINDxAI Lab is pleased to share that Dr. Yunmei Liu received this Kentucky Transportation Cabinet award to advance teen driver safety education in Kentucky.
  </p>
  <p>
    The project will examine ways to better understand teen driver safety and support the development of effective safety-focused education and intervention approaches for young drivers in Kentucky. The work reflects the MINDxAI Lab’s broader commitment to human-centered transportation research and safer communities.
  </p>
  <p>
    Dr. Liu will lead the project with Robert Kluger, Associate Professor in the University of Louisville’s Department of Civil Engineering, serving as Co-PI. The collaboration brings together expertise in human factors, transportation safety, and engineering to address an important challenge facing Kentucky families and communities.
