Dr. Yunmei Liu served as an **Associate Chair (AC) for the Full Papers Track** of the [16th International ACM Conference on Automotive User Interfaces and Interactive Vehicular Applications (AutoUI 2024)](https://www.auto-ui.org/24/). Her service supported the peer-review process for research on automotive interfaces and human interaction with vehicle technologies.

Following an invitation on April 21, 2024, Dr. Liu coordinated external reviews and contributed reviews and meta-reviews of submitted papers. This work helped the program chairs assess submissions and develop the conference's technical program. AutoUI's [reviewer guidelines](https://www.auto-ui.org/24/authors/reviewing/instructionsandtips/) describe how Associate Chairs support paper evaluation and synthesize reviewer feedback.

On August 12, the program chairs thanked Dr. Liu for her contributions as an Associate Chair following completion of the full-paper review process. This service reflects her engagement with the automotive human–computer interaction research community.
