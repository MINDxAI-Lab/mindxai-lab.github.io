  <p>
    The MINDxAI Lab is pleased to share that Dr. Yunmei Liu received a $10,000 Research Experiences for Undergraduates supplement from the National Science Foundation to support the lab’s ongoing CMMI project on cognitive-emotional state assessment and real-time automation intervention design for advanced driving systems.
  </p>
  <p>
    The supplement will support one undergraduate researcher during Summer and Fall 2026. The student will gain hands-on experience in human-centered AI, simulation-based experimentation, multimodal data processing, and transportation safety research while contributing to the continued development of the project’s experimental workflows.
  </p>
  <p>
    The undergraduate researcher will primarily assist with developing and refining virtual driving scenarios that represent safety-critical conditions and preparing multimodal experimental datasets for downstream modeling. These activities will help strengthen the realism, quality, and efficiency of the project’s simulation and data-analysis pipelines.
  </p>
  <p class="jr-grant-project">
    <b>Supported Project:</b> NSF CMMI Award 2535920, Collaborative Research: Exploring an Integrated Cognitive-Emotional State Assessment System to Support Real-Time Automation Intervention Design for Advanced Driving Systems.
