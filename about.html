ABOUT MINDxAI LAB

The Modeling Interaction, cogNition, and Decision-making x AI (MINDxAI) Lab is directed by Dr. Yunmei Liu in the Industrial and Systems Engineering Department at the University of Louisville.

Our research examines adaptive human–AI systems and mutual adaptation between people and AI over time. We combine experimental studies, wearable sensing, physiological data, behavioral analytics, machine learning, and statistical modeling to investigate cognition, emotion, decision-making, workload, situation awareness, and performance in human-in-the-loop systems.

Our application domains include transportation and automated driving, smart manufacturing, education and workforce development, rehabilitation and assistive technologies, and healthcare and biomedical AI. Across these domains, we aim to design systems that account for human strengths and limitations and improve safety, usability, and effectiveness.
