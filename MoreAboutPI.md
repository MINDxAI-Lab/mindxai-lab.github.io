    <h2 id="teaching-heading">Teaching</h2>

    <h3>Teaching Areas</h3>
    <p>
      Systems engineering; work and systems design; engineering probability and statistics; predictive analytics and machine learning; human factors and human-systems integration; human-AI teaming.
    </p>

    <h3>ISE 463/663 - Predictive Analytics for Decision Making II</h3>
    <p><em>Spring 2025 | Undergraduate/Graduate</em></p>
    <p>
      Developed a new course covering statistical learning, multilayer perceptrons, deep neural networks, CNNs, RNNs, optimization, and engineering applications in Python. A course project developed into a student first-author conference paper and an M.S. thesis research direction.
    </p>

    <h3>ISE 380 - Work and Systems Design</h3>
    <p><em>Fall 2024, Fall 2025, Fall 2026 | Undergraduate</em></p>
    <p>
      Redesigned the course around experiential learning, hands-on laboratories, and a field study with GE Appliances. Topics include time study, work sampling, ergonomics, workplace design, system safety, and hazard reduction. One student team transformed a course project into the first UofL undergraduate presentation accepted at an international HFES meeting.
    </p>

    <h3>ISE 360/560 - Probability and Statistics for Engineers</h3>
    <p><em>Spring 2024, Fall 2025, Fall 2026 | Undergraduate/Graduate</em></p>
    <p>
      Teach probability, random variables and distributions, estimation, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, and simple linear regression. Added engineering-based examples, in-class problem solving, and pre-exam workshops to strengthen engagement and mastery.
    </p>
